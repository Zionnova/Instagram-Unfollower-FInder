How to get your export from Instagram
  1. In the Instagram app, go to your profile, open the menu, then Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information (called Download your information in some versions).
  2. Create an export for your Instagram account and choose Export to device.
  3. Under Customize information, select only Followers and following. Everything else just makes the file bigger and slower to arrive.
  4. Set Date range to All time. The range filters by when each follow started. With "Last year", anyone who followed you before then is missing and shows up here by mistake.
  5. Set Format to JSON. HTML works too, but JSON includes the date of each follow, so you can sort by it.
  6. Start the export. Instagram emails you when it's ready, usually within minutes, sometimes a day. Download the .zip and drop it above.
