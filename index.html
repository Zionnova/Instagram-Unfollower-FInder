Getting the file from Instagram

  1. 1

    In the app, open Accounts Center

    Your information and permissions › Export your information. Some versions call it Download your information.

  2. 2

    Choose Export to device with these settings

    • Followers and following
    • All time
    • JSON
  3. 3

    Wait for the email, then download the .zip

    Usually a few minutes. Sometimes up to a day.

Pick “All time”. Shorter ranges leave out people who followed you years ago, and they’d show up here by mistake. HTML works too, but JSON has the dates.
